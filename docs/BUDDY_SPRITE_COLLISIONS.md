# Buddy sprite-name collisions (cross-engine)

## The problem

BuddyDoom is one engine with **one shared sprite namespace** (`sprnames_builtin[]`
in `files/info.c`, extended by the per-game `*_spr*.inc` includes). Doom, Heretic
and Hexen sprite codes were made mutually unique, but **Strife still uses its native
4-char codes**, and a handful of those duplicate a Doom/Heretic/Hexen name.

For a **co-op buddy** this matters: a buddy that borrows a monster's sprites and
ships them in its PWAD under the native name will **overwrite that other game's
sprite** when the pack is loaded there. The flagged example: a *Strife Stalker*
buddy ships `SPID*` frames, and loaded in **Doom** those replace the **Spider
Mastermind**.

## The solution (matches ZDoom)

ZDoom stores the Strife Stalker's frames as `SPID*` in `strife1.wad` but the actor
uses the collision-free sprite **`STLK`**, renaming `SPID*` -> `STLK*` at load. Do
the same here:

1. A buddy that borrows a colliding sprite ships its frames as **PNG under a unique
   placeholder base** (e.g. `STLK`) in the buddy WAD -- so it never clobbers a
   native sprite, and it works in **every** game.
2. The engine remaps the placeholder to the real art at load. BuddyDoom already has
   the hook: **`Strife_RemapNativeSprites()`** in `files/strife.c` -- currently a
   *Phase-1 no-op stub* ("point the placeholder S* sprite codes at strife1.wad's
   native 4-char codes"). Implementing it is the engine half of this fix.

## Sprites that need renaming

**21 cross-game collisions, all involving Strife** (Doom/Heretic/Hexen are already
unique among themselves):

### What this means for the Base-monster buddy feature

Of the sprite bases the "Base monster" picker assigns (`tools/mybuddy.cpp`
`MONSTERS[]`), **only `SPID` (Strife Stalker) collides**. Every other Strife monster
sprite is already unique and needs no rename:

`AGRD, ROB1, PGRD, ROB2, MLDR, PRST, SEWR, ROB3, PRGR, MNAM, ALN1, TURT, RATT`

`PLAY` also collides but is the intentional shared player sprite (the buddy default),
not something a buddy borrows from a monster.

## Next steps

1. **Engine:** pick placeholder codes for the colliding Strife sprites (start with
   `SPID` -> `STLK`, like ZDoom) in `strife_sprnames.inc`, and implement
   `Strife_RemapNativeSprites()` to alias each placeholder to its native lump before
   `R_Init`.
2. ~~**Buddy build:** when a buddy borrows a colliding monster's art, pack its frames
   as PNG under the placeholder base in the buddy WAD.~~ **Done for the Stalker:** its
   83 frames ship as `STLK*` PNGs in `buddydoom.wad` (source: `tools/wad.gfx/STLK*.png`)
   and the engine has a real `SPR_STLK` -- so the Stalker buddy renders in Doom, Heretic
   and Hexen too, and never touches the Spider Mastermind. Its sounds took the same
   treatment (`STLKSIT`/`STLKACT`/`STLKATK`/`STLKDTH`/`STLKFIR`), since `DSSPISIT` &
   friends would otherwise shadow strife1.wad's own lumps. `STLKFIR` is Freedoom's
   pistol rather than a Strife lump: the Stalker never fired, so there was no original
   gunshot to borrow, and a BSD-licensed asset is the better default for one we
   invented.
3. **Tool:** update `MONSTERS[]` in `tools/mybuddy.cpp` to hand out the placeholder
   base (e.g. Strife Stalker sprite `SPID` -> `STLK`) once the engine remap lands.

Note that step 2 and step 1 solve *different* halves: the buddy pack no longer collides
(done), but a Strife **map** still spawns `MT_S_STALKER` off `strife1.wad`'s native
`SPID*` -- that is what the remap in step 1 is for.
